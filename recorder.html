How to use it
  1. Open what you want to record (Meet, Zoom, Excel, Power BI, anything).
  2. Choose your options, then click Start Recording.
  3. Pick the tab, window, or screen in the browser's share picker. Turn on "Share tab audio" if you chose a tab.
  4. Use Pause if you need a break. Tap Mark to drop a chapter timestamp (e.g. "VLOOKUP demo starts") — it exports as a text file ready for your YouTube description. Stop when you're done.
  5. Download from the list below — recordings stay listed until you close this page.

Works in desktop Chrome, Edge or Brave. Camera bubble and mic need their own permission prompt the first time. Files save as .webm.
